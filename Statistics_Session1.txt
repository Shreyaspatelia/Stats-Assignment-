Task 4. Write a short paragraph explaining the difference between 'population' and 'sample' 
using your Zomato order data as an example. Mention which one is more practical to use for
a food delivery company and why.

In the Zomato order data, the population represents all 20 order amounts collected for the day, while a sample represents only a smaller part of the data, such as the first 5 orders. The population 
gives a complete picture, but analyzing a sample is usually more practical for a food delivery company
because the company may have thousands or millions of orders. A representative sample saves 
time and resources while still providing useful information about overall customer ordering patterns.
